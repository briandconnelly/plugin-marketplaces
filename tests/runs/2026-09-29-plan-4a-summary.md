## What these runs did and did not measure

- No arm ran `--remote`.
  In s5, all three arms examined v1.4.0 in the mirror, found its telemetry, and held the release, so no pin moved and there was nothing new to check upstream; each said it did not run the remote level because the task forbade contacting remote services.
  In s7, all three reported the CI run's remote job as inconclusive and said they could not run the remote level themselves for the same reason.
- So these runs show that the new wording caused no regression and that arms respected the remote-contact rule; they do not show an arm using the remote level, and plan 4a's measurement of `--remote` itself is the calibration in `docs/research/2026-09-29-remote-level-probes.md`, not these runs.
- A scenario that would exercise `--remote` needs a pin that moves and a local mirror the arm knows it may reach; s5's arms had the mirror but no reason to move the pin.
  That is a finding for the next plan's scenarios, not something these runs can settle.

## Isolation

- Every tool call of all six runs was read in full (3 to 6 calls each); no violation, and no `remote-fetch` flag.
- s5-r4's one flag is the checker reading a read-only `git --no-pager diff` in UPSTREAM as a write; s7-r6 validated a copy with a declared `marketplace-policy.json` inside `.tool-homes/` and deleted it.
- The only changes to the real tool homes were the Codex app's own daemon files, as in plans 2b to 3.
- A full rescan of every artefact line found no credential.
