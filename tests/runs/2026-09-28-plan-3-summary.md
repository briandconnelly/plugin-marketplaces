## Scenario 1: regression check

All three runs had full marks, against 4 of 6 in plan 2c (whose two failures were criterion 2, the regression the plan-2c refinement fixed), with a median of 17 tool calls and 164 s against 17 and 159 s.
Each arm kept `weather-mcp` in both catalogs, used a `url` source for Codex, and reported that the plugin's server cannot start in Codex because its `.mcp.json` passes `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` literally; two arms also reported the telemetry v1.4.0 adds.

- No trigger runs: SKILL.md's description did not change.
